# Emotion Cupcakes Menu

## Six Cupcake Flavors Inspired by Human Emotions

### 1. **Joyful Sunrise** ☀️
*Lemon-Lavender Bliss*

A bright lemon cupcake infused with delicate lavender, topped with honey-vanilla buttercream and a sprinkle of edible gold dust. This flavor captures the warm, uplifting feeling of pure joy—light, bright, and full of hope, like the first rays of morning sun breaking through the clouds.

---

### 2. **Melancholy Midnight** 🌙
*Dark Chocolate Sea Salt*

A rich, deep chocolate cupcake with a salted caramel core, finished with silky dark chocolate ganache and a touch of flaky sea salt. This flavor embodies the bittersweet beauty of melancholy—complex, introspective, and hauntingly delicious, like quiet moments under a starlit sky.

---

### 3. **Passionate Fire** 🔥
*Chili-Cinnamon Red Velvet*

A bold red velvet cupcake with a hint of cayenne and cinnamon, crowned with cream cheese frosting infused with Mexican vanilla. This flavor represents passionate intensity—warm, exciting, and unforgettable, with a lingering heat that awakens all your senses.

---

### 4. **Serene Meadow** 🌸
*Earl Grey Honey*

A delicate Earl Grey tea-infused cupcake with local honey, topped with light chamomile buttercream and a candied violet. This flavor captures peaceful serenity—calm, soothing, and gently floral, like a quiet afternoon walk through a sunlit meadow.

---

### 5. **Nostalgic Comfort** 🏠
*Brown Butter Vanilla Bean*

A tender vanilla bean cupcake made with nutty brown butter, frosted with classic vanilla buttercream and a sprinkle of cinnamon sugar. This flavor embodies warm nostalgia—familiar, comforting, and heartwarming, reminiscent of home-baked treats from childhood.

---

### 6. **Curious Wonder** ✨
*Matcha White Chocolate Raspberry*

A vibrant matcha green tea cupcake with white chocolate chips and a raspberry compote center, topped with white chocolate cream cheese frosting and freeze-dried raspberry dust. This flavor represents curiosity and wonder—unexpected, adventurous, and delightfully surprising, with layers that unfold with each bite.

---

*Each cupcake is freshly baked and designed to evoke a different emotional experience. Let your taste buds feel.*
